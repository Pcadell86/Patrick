## Business Overview

### Company Description

Clean Science and Technology is one of the fastest-growing and most profitable fine and specialty chemical companies globally. The company manufactures functionally critical specialty chemicals using unique catalytic chemistries and clean, eco-friendly manufacturing processes.

### Product Portfolio

#### 1. Performance Chemicals (~69% of Revenue)
- **MEHQ (Monomethyl Ether of Hydroquinone)** - Used as a polymerization inhibitor in acrylic acids, acrylic esters, and super absorbent polymers (diapers, sanitary pads)
- **BHA (Butylated Hydroxy Anisole)** - Anti-oxidant for food and feed industry
- **AP (Ascorbyl Palmitate)** - Used in infant food formulations, breakfast cereals, and cosmetics
- **TBHQ (Tertiary Butyl Hydroquinone)** - Stabilizer in the oil industry
- **HALS (Hindered Amine Light Stabilizers)** - UV stabilizers for paints, automotive coatings, outdoor furniture

#### 2. FMCG Chemicals
- **Anisole** - Applications in cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals
- **4-MAP** - UV blocker in sunscreens

#### 3. Pharmaceutical Intermediates
- **Guaiacol** - Pharmaceutical intermediate
- **DCC (Dicyclohexylcarbodiimide)** - Reagent for pharmaceutical synthesis

## Competitive Advantages (Economic Moat)

### 1. Proprietary Vapour-Phase Technology
Clean Science is the **only company globally** to successfully commercialize vapour-phase technology for manufacturing Anisole from phenol. This breakthrough technology provides:
- **Lowest manufacturing costs** in the industry
- Better atom economy
- Only water as effluent (environmentally friendly)
- Superior profit margins vs. competitors using liquid-phase processes

### 2. Backward Integration
- Fully integrated manufacturing for MEHQ and Guaiacol
- For HALS: One of very few companies starting from basic raw materials (acetone and ammonia)
- All catalysts developed entirely in-house

### 3. Process Innovation
Unlike peers who use hydroquinone to manufacture food antioxidant chemicals, Clean Science uses vapour-phase technology to manufacture anisole and then forward integrates to antioxidant products. This innovation results in:
- Higher profit margins
- Greater pricing power
- Difficult for competitors to replicate (4-5 years to scale)

### 4. Scale & Cost Leadership
- Economies of scale from being the largest global producer of multiple products
- Superior supply chain management
- Strategic location in India with competitive labor costs

### 5. "China Plus One" Beneficiary
- First-mover advantage as companies diversify away from China
- Benefit from rising European energy costs making Indian manufacturing more competitive
